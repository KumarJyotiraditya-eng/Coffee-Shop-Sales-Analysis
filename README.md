☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Business Intelligence Project



&#x20;📌 Project Overview



The Coffee Shop Sales Analysis project was developed to analyze transactional sales data and uncover actionable business insights. Using Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, the project transforms raw sales records into interactive dashboards that support data-driven decision-making.



The analysis focuses on revenue performance, transaction trends, customer purchasing behavior, store-wise performance, and operational efficiency.







&#x20;🎯 Business Problem



Coffee shop management lacked a centralized reporting solution to monitor sales performance, identify peak operating hours, and compare store-level performance.



The objective of this project was to develop an interactive Business Intelligence solution capable of:



* Monitoring revenue performance
* Identifying peak sales periods Comparing store performance
* Understanding customer purchasing behavior
* Generating actionable business recommendations

**🔍 Key Insights**



* Morning hours generated the highest revenue and transaction volume.
* Peak customer activity occurred between 8 AM and 10 AM.
* Hell's Kitchen emerged as the highest-performing store location.
* Evening hours experienced comparatively lower customer demand.
* Sales activity was concentrated within a few high-performing operational periods.







💡 **Business Recommendations**



**Morning Sales Optimization**



* Increase staffing during peak morning hours.
* Prioritize inventory replenishment before opening.



**Store Performance Enhancement**



* Replicate successful practices from top-performing stores.



**Evening Revenue Improvement**



* Introduce promotions and loyalty-based offers.



**Inventory Optimization**



* Align stock planning with hourly sales patterns.
